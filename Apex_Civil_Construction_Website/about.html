Our Background

Apex Civil & Construction Services is a fictional South African small-to-medium enterprise created for this academic website project. It serves homeowners, developers, businesses, contractors and community organisations.

The organisation focuses on building construction, civil works, roads and paving, renovations, maintenance and earthworks.
